      Assumptions

      • Guests use the hall bath (one at a time, ~2.5 min each). With 2 bathrooms set, Bed 3's bath opens too: guests pick whichever is quicker, and walking through Bed 3 adds ~20 s.
      • Front door is inferred from the plan (between coat closet and pantry). Check it on site.
      • Drinks follow the crowd profile: light (a round every 50–70 min, half BYOB), medium (35–50 min, 30% BYOB) or heavy (25–40 min, 10% BYOB). Rounds from our stock split 60% jungle juice, 22% beer, 18% White Claw, from your recipe.
      • Pouring from a dispenser takes 25–40 s, two at a time. Digging a beer out of the cooler takes 12–22 s, one at a time. A White Claw off the cart takes 10–20 s, two at a time.
      • Arrivals follow the crowd profile: early (70% in by 10:30), spread (half by 10:30, all by midnight) or late (half by 11, all by 12:30 last entry). Most grab a drink within 5 minutes of walking in. Closing: last pour 1:45, most leave 1:55–2:05.
      • Disco ball: 20 in ball hung just under the ceiling. A 6 ft guest's raised hand reaches about 8 ft.
      • UT Austin college crowd. Free cigarettes and weed on the balcony: by default half the guests step out every 25–45 min for 4–8 min. Vapes are allowed inside, so vapers stay put.
      • Built-in counters, fridge, pantry and closets are real obstacles even though they're drawn as closed blocks.
