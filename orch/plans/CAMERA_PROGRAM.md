# AFTERIMAGE 001 · Disposable camera program (DRAFT, nothing bought)

**Goal (5 cameras, decided 18 Oct):** 5 disposable cameras go round the guests during the night. We collect them at the end, develop the film and post the best frames. It fits the phones-away rule: the photos still happen, they just come back days later.

## 1. Which camera (prices are estimates from 18 Oct searches; check before buying)
| Option | Film | Look | Est. price each | 5 cameras | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Ilford XP2 Super single-use** (recommended) | B&W, ISO 400, **C-41 process** | Black and white, on brand | ~$20–30 | ~$100–150 | B&W film that any colour (C-41) lab develops fast and cheap. Matches the brand with no conversion. |
| B · Kodak Tri-X single-use | B&W, true B&W process | Classic grainy B&W | ~$18–26 | ~$90–130 | Needs a B&W-capable lab; slower and often mail-in. |
| C · Kodak FunSaver 800 (flash) | Colour, ISO 800 | Colour, we convert to B&W | ~$12–26 | ~$60–130 | Cheapest and easiest to find; ISO 800 suits a dark room. |
| D · Fujifilm QuickSnap Flash 400 | Colour, ISO 400 | Colour, we convert | ~$13–25 | ~$65–125 | Common; ISO 400 is a bit slow for a dim room. |

All have a built-in flash, which is the look of party disposables. 27 shots each, so about **135 frames** in total.

## 4. Hand-out rule (open: who runs it)
- The 5 guests who claimed one by DM ("CAMERA", first 5) get a camera at the door, with a numbered sticker (MOTION 01–05) that matches the camera.
- Sign-out on **paper at the door**, camera number to first name only. **It never goes in git or the repo** (guest data rule).
- Rule on the sticker: "Shoot, then pass it on. Don't take it home."

## 5. Collection
- **1:45 AM last pour:** one named crew member walks the room and counts all 5 back in by number.
- Cameras go in one sealed bag. Jovanni takes it home and drops it at the lab the next day.

## 6. Rights and posting
- The door sign and the crew script disclose the recording and the cameras (drafts in `media/posts/rules/`).
- **Opt-out:** anyone who tells a crew member they want to stay out of frame has their photos removed before posting.
- Review every frame before posting. No faces of people who opted out, nothing compromising, and nothing showing the venue address, street or building signage.
