Open-source tools

FFmpegThe engine under all of this: filters for blur, displace, xfade, minterpolate, lagfun, vid.stab. Blender3D logos (the Element 3D route), compositor glare and sun beams, tracking, particles. NatronNode compositor in the spirit of Nuke and After Effects; runs OpenFX plugins. G'MICHundreds of image filters: film emulation, halftone, pixel sort. KdenliveOpen-source editor with frei0r effects, keyframes and speed ramps. GlaxnimateVector motion graphics and easing, for animated type. RIFEAI frame interpolation for smooth slow motion (the Twixtor route). Real-ESRGANAI upscaling for low-res phone clips (the Topaz route). FFglitchReal datamosh by editing motion vectors. ntsc-rsAccurate VHS and analogue TV emulation. vid.stabStabilizes shaky footage (the Warp Stabilizer route). Every cut needs a reason

MotionThe action carries across the cut. Cut a third into the move.
Eye-traceThe subject sits in the same third of the frame on both sides.
GraphicShape or brightness rhymes: a diagonal arm into diagonal beams.
MusicLands on a kick, a bar line or a phrase change.
StorySomething new arrives: the name, the eyes, the date.

The arc of a teaser

Beats 0–6

The mark flies in. Its exit zooms into the first shot.

Beats 6–14

Build. Cuts every 2, then every beat. Letterbox opens wider.

Beats 14–17

The name, one word per beat.

Beat 18

One beat of black in the dip.

Beat 19

Drop: the best image, full frame, impact shake.

Beats 25–27

One hero speed ramp. Hold it.

Beats 27–35

Eyes, name, date, the mark.
