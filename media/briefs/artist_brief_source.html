The brief

Build it.
Don't peak it.

You're the first thing people hear. Take the room from people arriving with a drink to a full floor, then hand it to Glamz and on to HE//THEY warm, not spent.

  • 9:00–10:00 · arrivals. Groove first, room to talk. suggestion
  • 10:00–11:00 · the floor fills. Lift the energy toward garage and house.
  • 11:00–12:15 · b3b with Glamz. We'd like to agree how you trade: track for track, or blocks.
  • 12:00 · the designed peak. Lights go red, the eyes come on. Want a say in that track?
  • 12:15 · hand off to HE//THEY. Their lane is UKG, hard house / electro and bass house, mostly 134–140 BPM.
Timings and handoffs are ours to agree together. Nothing here is a set list.04
